Method

We read ads in Meta’s public Ad Library. Vera, an AI, read each ad’s words against Meta’s published ad rules and the FTC and FDA guidance, and gave each a tier and the rule it rests on. A tier and a rule show what Vera flagged; we do not know what Meta decided about any of these ads. Vera read each ad’s headline and the start of its text, not its picture or video or the page it leads to.
