Guarantees

If it doesn’t work for you.

30 days money back

Cancel within 30 days of your first payment and we refund that payment in full; once per brand.

Rejection guarantee

If Meta rejects one of your ads for a claim Vera reviewed (in a morning brief or a check before launch) and did not flag, that month’s fee is refunded or credited, your choice; it applies when the ad that ran is the text and media Vera reviewed and Meta’s notice names a claim-related policy; a claim Vera flagged, at any tier, that ran anyway is not covered.

Billing

Billing questions.

How am I billed?
Monthly, in advance, through Stripe.
Does it renew?
Renews monthly until you cancel; cancel any time in the billing portal or by email.
How do I cancel?
In the billing portal or by emailing hello@merchient.com. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid month: you keep access until then, and no further payment is taken.
What does the 30-day guarantee cover?
Cancel within 30 days of your first payment and we refund that payment in full; once per brand.
What if Meta rejects one of my ads?
See the rejection guarantee above: it applies when the ad that ran is the text and media Vera reviewed and Meta’s notice names a claim-related policy.
Is this legal advice?
No. This is risk review, not legal advice, and you stay responsible for your ads.
