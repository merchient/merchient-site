Your website. Vera reads your public ads and pages. No login, no access, nothing to install.
Is this legal advice?
No. Risk review, not legal advice. Vera names the rule a line trips, from Meta’s ad policies and FTC and FDA guidance. Whether an ad runs is Meta’s decision.
Do you read Amazon listings?
No. Amazon is not covered.
How do I cancel Vera?
In the billing portal or by emailing hello@merchient.com. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid month: you keep Vera until then, and no further payment is taken. Founding brands: cancel within 30 days of your first payment and we refund it in full; once per brand.
