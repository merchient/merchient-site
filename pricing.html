Billing

Billing questions.

When is an ad rescue invoiced?
After approval. A rescue is invoiced only once Meta has approved the fixed wording. The first one is not invoiced at all.
When is a listing invoiced?
Only once Amazon has reinstated it. Finding the reason and writing the corrected listing are never invoiced.
When is an account invoiced?
Only once the account is back. If Meta or Amazon keeps it restricted, nothing is invoiced.
When is a launch clearance invoiced?
Once your ads are approved and live, as one invoice for the launch.
When does the monthly plan start billing?
After the first result. The first month is invoiced after your first rescue or clearance has passed. After that, monthly, in advance.
Does it renew?
The monthly plan renews each month until you cancel; cancel any time in the billing portal or by email. Rescues, listings, accounts and clearances are one-off and never renew.
How do I cancel?
In the billing portal or by emailing hello@merchient.com. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid month: you keep access until then, and no further payment is taken.
What if the fix is rejected again?
Then it is not invoiced, and we look at it again with you.
What if the cause isn’t the claims?
We tell you the same day, and nothing is invoiced.
What counts as approved?
Meta or Amazon accepting the fixed version, reinstating the listing or restoring the account, as shown in your account or in their email. You tell us with one reply; a screenshot helps.
Is this a lawyer’s opinion?
No. This is risk review, and you stay responsible for your ads and listings.
