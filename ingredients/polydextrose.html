What can you say about polydextrose in your ads and on your site?

The library gives no supported wording for polydextrose, because no listed source supports one at a dose. Avoid wording that names or implies a disease, a drug or a treatment, such as “Relieves constipation, without ‘occasional’”.

Reviewed 14 October 2026

Not legal advice. This guide shows the wording that published evidence supports at a daily dose; it is not a ruling on any claim. Your own product, its form and dose, and your own evidence still need to match the wording you use.

Supported wordings

What you can say.

Claims Facts

No supported wording yet.

No listed source gives a use with a dose for polydextrose: no Health Canada monograph for it is in the monograph list, Regulation (EU) No 432/2012 has no claim for it, there is no ODS fact sheet among those read, and FDA lists no health claim. FDA’s dietary fiber question-and-answer page returned HTTP 404 on 2026-10-14, so FDA’s view of it as dietary fiber was not read.

What to leave out.
