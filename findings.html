HighFinding 1Example, not a real brand

Claims Facts

Where: on a product page

“Calms arthritis flare-ups in your knees and hands.”

The ruleR1 (FDA: 21 CFR 101.93), High
Who would act, and whyThe FDA. Arthritis is a disease, and the line says the product calms its flare-ups, which is treating it.
The Safe rewrite“Supports healthy joints.”
