Data deletion

Last updated 13 October 2026

Ask us to delete your data

Email hello@merchient.com with your brand name. Your data is deleted within 30 days, and we reply to confirm when it is done.

This covers the ad account data you shared with us, your reports and briefs, the public content we collected about your brand, and our emails with you. We keep only the billing records that tax law requires us to keep. The providers that process data for us, listed in our privacy policy, handle any copies they hold under their own terms.

Prospects, brands we have not worked with yet, can ask the same way.

Remove Merchient’s access in Meta

You can remove Merchient’s view-only access to your ad account at any time, without asking us. You need full control of your business portfolio.

  1. Go to Settings in Meta Business Suite for your business portfolio, at business.facebook.com/settings.
  2. Click Partners in the menu on the left, then select Merchient.
  3. Click Options in the top right, then select Remove from business portfolio.
  4. Click Remove partner.

These steps follow Meta’s Business Help Center article Remove partners from your business portfolio. Once access is removed, Vera can no longer read the account. Removing access does not delete the data we already hold; email us for that.
