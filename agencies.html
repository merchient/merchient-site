Agencies

The rescue desk for agencies.

Every client’s rejected ads, restricted ad accounts, pulled Amazon listings and Amazon account notices: send them to one address and get back the real reason and the corrected wording, the same day.

The first rescue for each client is free.

When a client wants Vera every day, you get a referral share, agreed in writing.

Send a client’s case

What it costs, per client.

  • An ad: The first rescue for each client is free. After that, $250 a rescue, and you pay only if the fixed wording is approved.
  • An Amazon listing: The reason and the corrected listing are free. The Plan of Action, written and followed until the listing is back: $500, and you pay only if it’s reinstated.
  • An account: The reason and the fixes are free. The full account review and the review request, followed until the account is back: $1,000, and you pay only if it comes back.

What Vera doesn’t fix: payment or identity problems, hacked accounts, counterfeit or intellectual-property complaints, and anything that isn’t about what the ad or listing says. No charge for those.

How to send a client’s case.

  1. Email akhil@merchient.com with the subject “Rescue” and your client’s brand name after it, for example “Rescue: (client’s brand)”.
  2. Say what happened: an ad rejected, an ad account restricted, a listing pulled, or an Amazon account notice.
  3. Attach the ad or listing (or paste its text), the notice Meta or Amazon sent, and the link to the page the ad sends people to.
  4. Say who the fix goes back to: you, or your client with you copied.

One address for every client. Each client is kept separate, and nothing about one client is shown to another.

What your client gets.

  • The real reason, quoting the rule it broke.
  • The corrected wording, keeping the hook your team wrote.
  • For an ad, an appeal draft; for a listing, the Plan of Action; for an account, the review request.

All of it comes back the same day, to you. You decide what reaches your client, and under whose name.

Vera every day.

Every new ad cleared the same day before it runs, your account watched every day, and the stronger claims you’re allowed. $1,500 a month, founding. Nothing is paid until Vera has got one of your ads through: the first month is invoiced after your first rescue or clearance has passed. 30 days money back.

Per client, after the first rescue or clearance. Under about $500 a day of ad spend, the per-result steps are all you need.

The referral share.

When a client wants the every-day plan, you get a referral share, agreed in writing before the client signs. Ask for the terms at akhil@merchient.com.

Small print: Risk review, not a lawyer’s opinion. No promise that Meta or Amazon will approve an ad, reinstate a listing or restore an account: they decide, and they act for reasons beyond claims.
