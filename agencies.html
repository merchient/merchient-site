Agencies

The same two, per client brand.

For each client brand: the 48-Hour Claims Audit, then Vera, your claims employee. The first three client brands at an agency are at the founding price. No percentage, no white label.

See the prices

The 48-Hour Claims Audit, per client.

Every live ad and page the client runs, read in 48 hours: the Red and Yellow lines, each with the rule in plain words and a safer line that keeps the sell; the category’s Winning Lines; the client’s Claim Gap. Read by Vera, checked by a person. $450, $750 or $1,200, by the client’s live ads. If it finds nothing Red or Yellow, it is refunded in full.

Vera, per client.

Every new ad and page pre-cleared before it runs, same-day rescues and appeals, the weekly watch, and Winning Lines and the Claim Gap every month. $1,500 a month for an agency’s first three client brands, then $2,500 a month. The audit’s price counts in full toward the first month (how).

How to send a client’s case.

  1. Email akhil@merchient.com with the subject “Rescue” and your client’s brand name after it, for example “Rescue: (client’s brand)”.
  2. Say what happened: an ad rejected, or an ad account restricted.
  3. Attach the ad (or paste its text), the notice Meta sent, and the link to the page the ad sends people to.
  4. Say who the fix goes back to: you, or your client with you copied.

One address for every client. Each client is kept separate, and nothing about one client is shown to another.

What your client gets.

  • The real reason, quoting the rule it trips.
  • The corrected wording, keeping the hook your team wrote.
  • For an ad, an appeal draft; for an account, the review request.

For a client on Vera, all of it comes back to you within one US business day. You decide what reaches your client.

Small print: Risk review, not legal advice. Amazon is not covered. No promise that Meta runs an ad or restores an account: Meta decides, and acts for reasons beyond claims.
