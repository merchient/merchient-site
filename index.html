Questions

Is Vera an AI?

Yes. Vera is an AI employee, Merchient’s claims employee. A person checks every report before it reaches you.

What access do you need?

None for the free report: it reads only your public ads and pages. After you join, view-only access to your Meta ad account, which takes two minutes to share. Nothing to log in to, no software. Vera never posts, edits or pauses anything in your accounts: she reads, and she drafts. You can remove the access at any time in Meta Business Settings.

Is this legal advice?

No. This is risk review, not legal advice, and you stay responsible for your ads. There is also no promise that an account is never restricted, because Meta acts for reasons beyond claims.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Month to month, cancel any time; cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid month. There is also 30 days money back, no questions asked, once per brand.

What do you do with my data?

We use it only for your reports, briefs and replies, and we never sell it. Anthropic (for Claude), Google Cloud, Google Workspace and Stripe process it for us. To get a copy or have it deleted, email hello@merchient.com. The privacy policy and the data deletion page have the details.
