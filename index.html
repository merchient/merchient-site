Claims Facts

Serving size: 1 ad line

Category: gut · Fictional example

“Bloated again? This probiotic heals leaky gut in 14 days.”

Risky phraseWho would act
“Bloated again?”asks about the viewer’s health, against Meta’s personal attributes policyMeta
“heals leaky gut”reads as a treatment claim, which a supplement can’t makeFDA
“in 14 days”needs competent and reliable scientific evidenceFTC

Safe rewrite, keeps the hook

“14 mornings, one new habit: a daily probiotic to support digestive health.*”

*These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Claims Facts

Serving size: 1 ad line

Category: sleep · Fictional example

“Can’t sleep? Cure insomnia naturally and fall asleep in 10 minutes.”

Risky phraseWho would act
“Can’t sleep?”implies the viewer has a health conditionMeta
“Cure insomnia”insomnia is a disease, so this is a drug claimFDA
“in 10 minutes”a specific result that needs proofFTC

Safe rewrite, keeps the hook

“Your 10-minute wind-down: magnesium and L-theanine to support restful sleep.*”

*These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Questions

Short answers.

Is Vera a person?
No. Vera is an AI employee. Akhil reviews the findings before every first report.
Can you fix an ad that was already rejected?
Yes, when the claims caused the rejection. Send it through the rescue form, or email the ad, the notice and the landing page link to akhil@merchient.com with the subject “Rescue”. It is free for up to 3 ads in your first month: here is what comes back and when.
What does the free scan look like?
An email with the riskiest line Vera found, why it’s a risk, a safer rewrite, and how many more like it she found. Reply, and you get the free first report, like this sample report for a fictional brand: the three things that matter most, each with who would act on it and a Safe and a Bold rewrite, then every other risky claim on request.
What access do you need?
None for the free scan or the free report: both read only your public ads and pages. After you join, view-only access to your Meta ad account, which takes two minutes to share: connect your ad account. Nothing to log in to, no software. On Meta, Vera only reads: view-only access. In your Shopify store, she changes nothing unless you turn on fixes and tap Apply on one. You can remove the access at any time in Meta Business Suite.
Is this legal advice?
No. This is risk review, not legal advice, and you stay responsible for your ads.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Renews monthly until you cancel; cancel any time in the billing portal or by email, and cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid month. Cancel within 30 days of your first payment and we refund that payment in full; once per brand.
What do you do with my data?
We use it only to run your scan and write your reports, briefs and replies, and, before you join, for one short outreach email and up to two follow-ups, which stop when you reply “no”. What you send us is kept encrypted on Google Cloud, in the United States. We never sell it. Anthropic processes it through Claude, Google hosts it and runs our email, and Stripe handles payments; once Vera sends emails herself, a sending service will process them too. To get a copy or have it deleted, email hello@merchient.com. The privacy policy and the data deletion page have the details.
