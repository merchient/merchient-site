# merchient.com

The website of Merchient, a trade name of Orbline LLC: claims review for US supplement brands running Meta ads. Contact: hello@merchient.com.

It is a static site: plain HTML pages and one stylesheet, with no cookies and no tracking. The only JavaScript is `rescue.js` on the rescue page and the home page (the form, and the live rescue count from api.merchient.com), `hero.js` on the home page (the Claims Facts panel from api.merchient.com), `configurator.js` on the home page and /pricing (the price from three answers, with no network call), and `scan.js` while the scan flag in `build.py` is switched on. One small script, `build.py`, generates some of the pages before they are committed.

## Pages

- `index.html`: the main page, in eight screens (site 51): the hook, a two-row sample, Winning Lines, the configurator, Vera, the founder and the rule books, the questions, the close. `build.py` writes three blocks into it: the configurator and Vera's screen (also on /pricing) and the Winning Lines rows from `data/winning-lines-gut.json`. The preview box is folded under the sample; it opens for `#scan` and `?site=`. `hero.js` turns that box into "Your website": it asks api.merchient.com for that website's Claims Facts panel (queued and polled when it is not read yet) and draws it in the page; when the service is busy or does not answer, or without JavaScript, the scan form shows instead. `index.html?site=brand.com` fills the box and asks for the panel once on load (for outreach links); a site the service refuses leaves the plain box. `?v=b` swaps the hero's headline and sentence for the words in their `data-b` attributes, when the page has them, and `v` goes with the panel requests. No other query key is read
- `method.html`, served at /method: "How Vera decides" (site 46): the two questions, who would act, Red/Yellow/Green, CONFIRMED and GUESS, what Vera read and did not, what she never says, how her own work goes out (a policy that is built but not running). Written by `python3 tools/build_method.py` from `~/code/merchient-content/copy/method/method.md` (read only; the Source columns and inline source marks are left out). Linked from the home page's Vera section and every page's footer; in the sitemap
- `cases/`: the numbered list of case files (`cases/index.html`) and `cases/0000/index.html`, case file #0, our own first ad (site 46), both written by `python3 tools/build_method.py` from `~/code/merchient-content/copy/cases/case-0000.md` (read only). "Case files" is in every page's footer; both are in the sitemap. `python3 tools/build_method.py --check` fails if a page is out of date
- `rescue.html`, served at /rescue: the free rescue form for a rejected ad or a pulled listing, its limits, and a free account checklist. `rescue.js` asks the rescue service at api.merchient.com for a form token and posts the form there; if the service does not answer, or the browser runs no JavaScript, the form opens an email to akhil@merchient.com with the subject "Rescue" instead
- `claims-watch.html`, served at /claims-watch: public enforcement and policy items on supplement claims, each with its date, who acted, what it means and the source
- `connect.html`: what to do to connect each surface Vera 360 reads, including sharing a Meta ad account view-only
- `start.html`, served at /start: what happens after a brand pays, in order, what Vera checks today and what she does not read, and the limits
- `pricing.html`, served at /pricing: the configurator (`#audit`) and Vera's screen (`#vera`), the same blocks as the home page. The checkout and booking links come from `config/links.json`; `python3 tools/configurator_selftest.py` checks the prices, the links and that no price shows outside the cards
- `agencies.html`, served at /agencies: the rescue desk for agencies
- `sample-report.html`: a fictional brand's free first report, as Vera writes one
- `vera-month.html`: a fictional training brand's month with Vera
- `findings.html`: example findings, on lines invented in the style of supplement product pages (not real brands)
- `ingredients/`: what a supplement brand can say about each ingredient, one page per ingredient and an index, generated by `build.py` from `data/`
- `privacy.html`: privacy policy
- `terms.html`: terms of service
- `data-deletion.html`: how to have data deleted and remove Merchient's access in Meta
- `security.html`: what Vera reads and never does, where data lives, and how to delete it
- `welcome.html`: shown after payment, with the next steps (not indexed)
- `shopify/connected.html`: the page a store owner lands on after connecting a Shopify store
- `404.html`: shown for addresses that do not exist
- `fonts/`: Archivo and Archivo Narrow, self-hosted, with their SIL Open Font License files
- `og-image.png`: the image shown when a page is shared
- `data/`: the ingredient guides, as JSON, and `sweeps.json`, this week's count of supplement ads read in Meta's ad library, which `build.py` writes into the line under the home page's hero
- `winning-lines/`: lines from ads in Meta's public Ad Library, longest-running first, one page for each category and a list page, generated by `build.py` from `data/winning-lines-*.json`; `data/survival-bands.json` is the days-running table (median and quartiles by category and tier) shown in the November ad index issue
- `build.py`: builds the ingredient guides and the sitemap, and writes the number of founding places left into the pages

## How it is built

`build.py` also switches the home page’s scan box between opening the "Free scan request" email and posting to the scan service (`SCAN_API_ON`), and writes `scan.js` only while that is on. It writes the number of founding places left (`FOUNDING_PLACES_LEFT`, at the top of the file) into the pages that show it, generates `ingredients/` from the JSON in `data/`, and lists the guides in `sitemap.xml`. After changing either, run `python3 build.py` and commit what it writes. `python3 build.py --check` changes nothing and fails if a page is out of date. A number in the copy written as `{{field}}` is filled from `data/index-2026-11.json` (`{{index.ads_read}}`) or the mining lane's `survival-number.json` (`{{survival.tiers.Red.median_days}}`) and kept as `<span data-field="...">`, so the next build refills it; `--check` also fails while any `{{field}}` is left unfilled.

## How it is published

GitHub Pages serves the `main` branch of this repository, from the root folder, at https://merchient.com, the custom domain set in `CNAME`. The `.nojekyll` file makes GitHub Pages serve the files as they are. Every push to `main` publishes the site.
