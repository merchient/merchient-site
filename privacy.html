Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

This policy explains what Merchient collects, what we use it for, who processes it for us, how long we keep it, and how to get a copy or have it deleted.

Who we are

Merchient is a trade name of Orbline LLC, 30 N Gould St, STE R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. In this policy, “we” means Orbline LLC, trading as Merchient. You can reach us at hello@merchient.com.

What we collect

From brands that use Merchient

  • Contact details: the names and email addresses of the people we work with.
  • Billing details, through Stripe. Stripe collects your card details; we do not store card numbers.
  • The ad account data you share with us, view-only, through Meta: your ads, spend, review status and landing URLs.
  • Your public pages and reviews.
  • The emails you send us, including photos of your label and creators’ scripts and captions.
  • A read-only Klaviyo API key, if you connect email and SMS, and the emails and texts it lets Vera read. This surface is coming soon.
  • Read-only access to your Shopify store, through an install you approve in Shopify: products, pages, inventory and discounts, and, only if you turn on fixes, permission to change product and page text when you tap Apply (see Your Shopify store).
  • A review-app key, if you give us one, and the reviews it lets Vera read. Connecting a review app is coming soon.
  • The sentences you check with our Chrome extension, and the sign-in code if you signed in (see Check with Vera in Chrome).
  • Your email address, if you ask for Claims Watch on the Claims Watch page. You are on the list only after you answer our confirmation email. We use the address only to send Claims Watch, never sell it, and remove it when you reply “stop”.

From prospects

Prospects are brands we have not worked with yet. From them we collect:

  • Public content from the Meta Ad Library and from the brand’s website.
  • A business email address.
  • The website and work email you type into our scan form, once our scan service is on. Until then, the form opens an email to us, and we receive only what you send in it.

For the competitor claims watch, we also read the public Meta Ad Library ads of a brand’s rivals.

When you book a call, we receive your name, your email address and the time you choose, through Google Calendar, part of Google Workspace.

From the rescue form

When you send us a rejected ad or a pulled listing through our rescue form, we collect:

  • Your name, email address and brand, and your store’s address.
  • The ad’s text, or the listing’s title and bullets, and where it was rejected.
  • The platform’s notice, as it was sent to you.
  • Up to 3 screenshots.

Your Shopify store

What we read

When you install Merchient in Shopify, you approve read-only access to four things in your store:

  • Products: each product’s title, description, SEO description and details such as its label text, and your collections.
  • Pages: your store’s pages.
  • Inventory: which products are stocked.
  • Discounts: the discount codes your store runs, so we can check the codes your pages and emails promise.

We do not ask Shopify for your customers or your orders, and we never receive them. We never ask for your password.

“Check with Vera”

When someone on your team checks a product with “Check with Vera” in the product editor, we read that product’s title, description and SEO description, and keep the check: the text as checked, the lines found and the rewrites. Shopify tells us which staff account asked, so the check can be shown to your store only.

Fixes, only if you turn them on

Fixes are off until you turn them on. When you do, Shopify asks you to approve two more permissions: to change product and page text. Vera uses them only when someone taps Apply on one fix. Each tap changes one field, only if the text on your store is still the text shown, and Undo puts it back.

For each fix we keep the old and the new text, and a record of each tap: when, who tapped (a staff account ID or the link’s label), and whether it changed anything. The record holds no page text.

Turning fixes off gives the two permissions back to Shopify. Reading continues until you uninstall.

When you uninstall

  • At once: Shopify tells us the app was uninstalled, and we stop reading your store.
  • After 48 hours: Shopify sends us a request to delete your store’s data. We then delete your store’s access tokens, your checks, your fix records, and our record of the install and of any billing through Shopify.
  • If another Merchient connection is still installed on the same store, only the uninstalled app’s access is deleted, and the rest stays until that one is removed too.

If you connected your store through a link from Merchient rather than from the Shopify App Store, uninstalling does not send us that request. Email hello@merchient.com with your brand name, and your data is deleted within 30 days.

Shopify’s privacy requests

Shopify sends every app three privacy requests, and we answer each one:

  • A customer asks for their data: we hold no customer data from Shopify. We write that down, and a person sends it to you, the store owner, within 30 days.
  • A customer asks to be deleted: we hold nothing about them from Shopify, so there is nothing to delete. We record that the request came.
  • Your store’s data is to be deleted (48 hours after you uninstall): as above.

We act on a request only when it is signed by Shopify for one of our apps, and once per request. Our record of each request holds no customer’s name, email or phone, and no store address.

How long we keep it

  • While the app is installed: what we read, your checks and your fix records, so you can see what changed and undo it.
  • After you uninstall: deleted as above.
  • Kept after deletion: a count of what we did for your brand (for example, the number of checks in a month), for billing. It holds no text from your store. Our record of each privacy request is kept too, as above.

Your data is kept encrypted, in the US. We never sell it.

Check with Vera in Chrome

“Check with Vera” is also a Chrome extension. It adds a button beside the text you write in Meta Ads Manager, Shopify admin and Klaviyo, and shows each sentence as Red, Yellow or Green, with the reason and safer wording. It is separate from the Shopify install described under Your Shopify store: the extension needs no Shopify permission.

What it sends, and when

  • The sentences in the field you check, only when you click “Check with Vera”: up to 8 sentences, each up to 300 characters. Longer fields are cut there, and the rest is not sent.
  • Which editor the field is in (Meta, Shopify or Klaviyo). The extension works this out from the page’s host, so Vera can judge an ad as an ad and an email as an email. The host itself and the page’s address are not sent.
  • Your sign-in, if you signed in: the code in your sign-in link, so the check counts toward your brand’s daily checks. Without it, the check counts toward the limit for your internet address.

Everything is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Nothing is sent while you type, or when you click into a field. Like any request over the internet, yours reaches our server with your internet address, which we use only as described under “How long we keep it” below.

What it never reads

The extension reads only the field you click into, and its text only when you click the button. To decide whether to show the button, it looks at that field’s own label, in your browser; the label is not sent. It reads nothing else on the page, and it runs only on Meta Ads Manager, Shopify admin and Klaviyo.

Who receives it

Merchient, at api.merchient.com, a server we run on Google Cloud in the United States (see Who processes it for us). Vera reads each sentence to give her verdict, the reason and safer wording, and the sentences are analysed by an AI model provider, Anthropic’s Claude. We do not sell what you send, we use it for nothing but your check, and we do not share it with Meta, Shopify or Klaviyo.

What stays in your browser

Only your sign-in (the code from your link and your brand’s name), in Chrome’s session memory, which is cleared when Chrome closes. The extension keeps no history, sets no cookies and has no analytics.

How long we keep it

  • The sentences and Vera’s answers: deleted 30 days after the check.
  • A record that the check was made: its number and time, never the text. Deleted after 30 days.
  • A code of your internet address, kept to apply the daily limits: a one-way, keyed code, never the address itself. Deleted after 30 days.

Signing out

Use “Sign out” in the extension. To end your sign-in link everywhere, email hello@merchient.com and we will replace it.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

We use what the extension handles only to give you the check. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it or transfer it to advertising platforms, data brokers or anyone judging credit. Our use of this information adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we use it for

We use this data only for these things:

  • Reports, briefs and replies: checking claims, sending reports and morning briefs, and answering emails.
  • Your scan: we use the website and work email from the scan form to run your scan and send it to you.
  • Your rescue: we use what you send through the rescue form to answer your rescue. We use it, anonymised, to improve Vera only if you tick the box on the form that says we may.
  • Outreach to prospects: we use a prospect’s public pages and business email address to send one short outreach email and up to two follow-ups. They stop when you reply “no”.
  • Checks in Chrome: we use the sentences you check with the extension only to give you that check.
  • Billing: we use billing details only to bill you.

We never sell your data.

Who processes it for us

These providers process data for us. Each handles it under its own terms and privacy policy, linked below. We make no promise about whether they use data for training, or how long they keep it, beyond what their own terms say.

  • Anthropic provides Claude, the AI model Vera runs on. The content Vera checks is sent to Claude so she can read it and draft reports. Label files you upload are read by an AI model provider (Anthropic) to record your Supplement Facts, and are not kept longer than the record needs. Anthropic privacy policy
  • Google Cloud hosts the server Vera runs on, in the United States, where what you send us is kept encrypted. Google Cloud Privacy Notice
  • Google Workspace runs our email, including hello@merchient.com. Google Cloud Privacy Notice (it covers Google Workspace)
  • Stripe takes payments and handles billing. Stripe privacy policy
  • A sending service will deliver Vera’s emails once she sends them herself.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no tracking or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. When a GitHub Pages site is visited, GitHub logs the visitor’s IP address for security purposes. GitHub privacy statement

How long we keep data

  • Brands: while the subscription runs. We delete the data within 30 days after it ends.
  • Prospects: up to 12 months after we collect it, unless the brand becomes a customer.
  • Rescues: what you send through the rescue form, 180 days after the rescue closes, and never more than 365 days after it arrived.
  • Checks in Chrome: the sentences, Vera’s answers, the record of each check and the code of your internet address, 30 days after the check.
  • Claims Watch: your email address, until you reply “stop” or ask us to remove it.
  • Billing records: as long as US tax and accounting law requires.

You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time.

Getting a copy, or deletion

To ask for a copy of your data, or for its deletion, email hello@merchient.com with your brand name. We send the copy, or delete the data, within 30 days. To have a rescue deleted at once, email akhil@merchient.com with the subject “Delete my rescue”. Revoking a Klaviyo key, a review-app key or the Shopify install in that service stops us reading from it; it does not delete what we already hold, except that uninstalling from Shopify starts the deletion described in Your Shopify store. The data deletion page also explains how to remove Merchient’s access in Meta.
