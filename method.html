Method

How Vera decides.

Every line in your ads and pages gets the same two questions. Every flag then gets a colour, the rule it rests on, and a basis that says how sure the reading is.

The two questions

#QuestionIn plain words
1Does the line break a written rule?We read it against the written rules of Meta, the FTC and the FDA.
2Does anyone act on it?A rule on paper is not the same as a rule someone enforces. Each flag says who would act on it: Meta, a regulator, or only the rule book.

Who would act

Four answers, highest first. These four names, and no others, appear under every flag.

Who would actWhen
Meta will likely actA Meta rule that Meta's ad review applies, flagged high with high confidence, in an ad.
Meta could actA Meta rule at medium or low severity, a less certain call, or a Meta passage that is general or that Meta acts on only after complaints. A line on the page an ad links to is at most this.
A regulator could actThe FTC's or the FDA's rules only, or a line that Meta's rule does not cover.
Rule book onlyA call by analogy, a doubt the run recorded, or no passage to cite. It never leads a report.

Red, Yellow, Green

ColourWhen
RedMeta will likely act, or a regulator could act on a high-severity flag.
YellowEvery other flag.
GreenA line that was checked and nothing was found, shown only where a list of checked lines helps.

The colour is worked out from who would act and how serious the flag is, by a script, never by eye. The sample report labels its line with nothing found "Clear".

CONFIRMED and GUESS

Each flag carries a basis in three parts. Each part is CONFIRMED or GUESS.

PartCONFIRMED whenGUESS when
The quoteThe exact sentence is word for word in the saved ad or page.A visual is described, the line could not be read, or there are no quoted words.
The ruleThe rule cited is a heading in the rule book.The rule cited is not one.
The readingThe rule's trigger words are in the quoted words.Anything inferred: a dose, an intent, an implied claim, missing evidence, or a page read only in part.
The whole flagEvery part is CONFIRMED.Any one part is a GUESS.

The basis is in the flags tables, the kit record and the dossier export, never in a cold message. It is not in the sample report. Case file #0 shows the reading on every line.

What we read

As the home page says it.

WhatIn plain words
Live adsEvery live ad.
Landing pagesEvery landing page the ads send people to.
Before it runsEvery new ad and page read against the three rule books, with a safer line for each flag.
While it runsThe weekly watch on your live ads, and Winning Lines and your Claim Gap every month.
When something slipsA pulled ad or a restricted account on a claims reason: the rewrite and the appeal letter, the same day.
Meta's advertising standardsAn ad may not say or imply that it knows a viewer's health, and may not show a before-and-after or a result that is unlikely.
The FTCA health claim needs sound scientific proof behind it, and a customer's result in an ad counts as the brand's own claim.
The FDAA supplement may say how it supports the body. A line that says it treats, cures or prevents a disease makes it a drug.
Public onlyPublic ads and pages: no login, no access to give.
Words, not imagesThe audit reads the words in your ads and pages, not the images.

What Vera never says

NeverWhy
"approved"A clean reading means no flags against the written rules. It never means safe or approved, or that no review will happen.
"ban-proof"Many bans come from tools, payments and security, not claims. Every promise is kept to claim-caused rejections and restrictions.
"FTC-compliant"A reading is a risk review against written rules, not a ruling on the law. No safer line says "legally allowed" or "FDA approved", and no alert says an ad is "non-compliant".
"replaces your lawyer"What Vera is not: a lawyer. The case file is a risk review, not a lawyer's sign-off.
A forecast of Meta's decisionA tier or a band reads the written rules. It is never a prediction of Meta's decision.
A result in numbersNo result in numbers, and no word that Meta or Amazon will approve anything.
An edit to your ads or your accountVera never edits an ad or touches your account. She reads; you decide.

How Vera's own work goes out

Once it is switched on, each piece of work will be sorted by how far it reaches if it is wrong.

KindFor exampleHow it will go out, once switched on
ContainedA finding, a rewrite, a briefWith no person, carrying its source and what was and was not checked. Work with no source, or no words, is held.
WideA fix on a live page, an email to a clientThree checks first: no new claim, the word pass, and the text check. A check that did not run counts as failed. Then a Red item needs a person; a missing tier counts as Red.
ClosedAn appeal to Meta or Amazon, money, anything in a client's accountAlways a person. No score, check or past approval opens it.
