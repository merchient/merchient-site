Security and data

Last updated 15 October 2026

What Vera reads, what she never does, where your data lives, who processes it, and how to have it deleted.

What Vera reads

  • Public pages, before you join: your website, your customer reviews and the ads Meta shows publicly in its Ad Library.
  • Your ad account, only after you share it with Merchient, view-only: your ads, spend, review status and landing URLs. How to share it.

What Vera never does

  • Post, edit or pause anything. Vera never posts, edits or pauses anything in your accounts: she reads, and she drafts.
  • Spend your money. View-only access cannot create or edit ads, and it gives no control of the ad account’s settings, finances or permissions.
  • Log in as you. There is nothing to log in to, and we never ask for your password.

Where your data lives

  • Google Cloud, in the United States: the server Vera runs on.
  • Google Workspace: our email, hello@merchient.com, and the page where you book a call.
  • Stripe: payments and billing. We do not store card numbers.

Who processes it

Anthropic processes the content Vera checks, through Claude, the AI model Vera runs on. Each provider handles data under its own terms, and our privacy policy links them. We never sell your data.

Deleting your data

Email hello@merchient.com with your brand name, and your data is deleted within 30 days. You can also remove Merchient’s access in Meta at any time. The data deletion page has the steps.
