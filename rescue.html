Send us a rejected ad.

Meta or Amazon turned down an ad over its claims? Send it to us. We tell you why, fix the wording and draft your appeal, free.

Free rescue

What to sendA screenshot of the ad, the rejection notice from Meta or Amazon, and the link to the landing page. Email them to akhil@merchient.com with the subject “Rescue”. Do not send passwords or logins; we never need them.
What comes backThe real reason it was rejected, in plain words; a fixed version that keeps the hook; and a draft of your appeal.
WhenThe same day, on US business days.
CostFree: your last rejected ad, fixed the same day. You do not need to join.
What we coverRejections and restrictions caused by the claims in your words. A rejection that comes from payments, tools or account security is outside what we fix; if that is what your notice says, we tell you.
After thatWe never act in your accounts. You submit the fixed ad and the appeal yourself, and Meta or Amazon decides.
PrivateWhat you send is used only to fix your ad and write your reply. We do not sell it, and we do not show your ad or name your brand anywhere without your permission. Email hello@merchient.com to have it deleted. Our privacy policy lists the providers that process it.
