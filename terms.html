Last updated 16 October 2026

These terms are between Orbline LLC, trading as Merchient, 30 N Gould St, STE R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA (“Merchient”, “we”), and the brand that subscribes (“the brand”).

1. The service

Merchient is for US supplement brands running Meta ads. Vera is Merchient’s claims employee. She is an AI employee. Akhil reviews findings before the first report and spot-checks every brief.

The brand gets:

  1. a free scan before paying anything: Vera checks every claim she reads against Meta’s ad policies and FTC and FDA guidance, and emails the riskiest line, why, a safer rewrite and how many more like it she found; replying to it gets a free first report: each risky claim quoted, with who would act on it (Meta, the FTC or the FDA) and a safe rewrite that keeps the hook;
  2. a brief from Vera every morning: new or changed risks, or a one-line all-clear;
  3. ad checks before launch: any new ad sent to Vera gets a verdict and a safe rewrite the same day;
  4. every week, what changed in Meta, FTC and FDA rules and what it means for the brand’s ads;
  5. bonus: a competitor claims watch, showing which claims rivals run and which ones disappeared;
  6. Akhil reviews findings before the first report and spot-checks every brief.

The free scan and the free first report read only the brand’s public ads and pages, so they need no access. After the brand joins, it takes two minutes to share the ad account with Merchient, view-only. Nothing to log in to, no software. Merchient delivers the full first report within 48 hours of connecting, counted from the share.

2. Founding terms

Month to month, cancel any time. 5 founding places, because Akhil reviews findings before the first report and spot-checks every brief. In return for the founding price: an honest testimonial after 30 days, if the brand is happy.

3. Guarantees

Cancel within 30 days of your first payment and we refund that payment in full; once per brand.

Rejection guarantee: if Meta rejects one of the brand’s ads for a claim Vera reviewed (in a morning brief or a check before launch) and did not flag, that month’s fee is refunded or credited, the brand’s choice; it applies when the ad that ran is the text and media Vera reviewed and Meta’s notice names a claim-related policy; a claim Vera flagged, at any tier, that ran anyway is not covered.

To use either guarantee, email hello@merchient.com.
