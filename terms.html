Terms of service

Last updated 4 November 2026

These terms are between Orbline LLC, trading as Merchient, 30 N Gould St, STE R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA (“Merchient”, “we”), and the brand that orders or subscribes (“the brand”).

1. The service

Merchient is for US supplement brands running Meta ads. Vera is Merchient’s claims employee. She is an AI employee, and a person checks her work.

There are two offers: the 48-Hour Claims Audit, and Vera. Every brand starts with the audit. The audit reads the brand’s public ads and pages, so it needs no access to the brand’s accounts.

2. The 48-Hour Claims Audit

Merchient reads every live ad and page the brand runs and delivers a numbered case file with the brand’s name on it. Read by Vera, checked by a person. The case file has:

  1. each Red and Yellow line, one line each: the line, the rule it trips in plain words, and a safer line that keeps the sell (lines that read fine are counted, not listed);
  2. the Winning Lines: the lines that run longest in the brand’s category, with how long each has run;
  3. the brand’s Claim Gap: the lines its category runs that the brand does not.

The price is one price per brand, by the number of live ads on the day of the order:

  • up to 25 live ads: $450;
  • 26 to 100 live ads: $750;
  • more than 100 live ads: $1,200.

The audit is paid at order, by card. Merchient delivers the case file within 48 hours of the order, on US business days.

If the audit finds nothing Red or Yellow, the price is refunded in full, and the brand keeps the Winning Lines.

3. Vera, your claims employee

Vera does the work every day. The brand decides. Each day she covers:

  1. every new ad and page, pre-cleared before it runs;
  2. same-day rescues and appeals: when an ad is pulled or the account is restricted on a claims reason, the rewrite and the appeal, written the same day (the guarantee in section 4 is the commitment);
  3. the weekly watch of the brand’s live ads and pages;
  4. Winning Lines and the Claim Gap, every month;
  5. a launch-day pre-clear for every new product;
  6. a 20-minute call with the founder, every month.

The founding ten. $1,500 a month for the first ten brands, kept for twelve months while the subscription runs. In exchange the brand gives:

  1. a case file, named or anonymous, at the brand’s choice;
  2. one sentence Merchient may quote, with the founding price shown beside it.

The founding ten closes at ten brands or on 30 November 2026, whichever comes first.

After the ten. $2,500 a month.

Vera is billed monthly, in advance, through Stripe. Every brand starts with the audit; section 5 says how its price counts toward Vera.

4. Guarantees

The founding guarantees, for the founding ten:

30 days money back: cancel within 30 days of your first payment and we refund that payment in full; once per brand.

The rescue guarantee: a pulled ad or a restricted ad account on a claims reason gets its rewrite and appeal within one US business day, or that month is refunded.

After the ten, the rescue guarantee alone, as above. The brand can cancel any time (section 6).

To use a guarantee, email hello@merchient.com. The audit’s refund is in section 2.

5. From the audit to Vera: the credit

The audit’s price counts in full toward the first month of Vera when the subscription starts within 14 days of the audit’s delivery.

6. Billing and cancellation

Vera renews each month until the brand cancels it. The brand can cancel any time, in the billing portal at billing.stripe.com/p/login/9B6eVd9WubnK6IOcMBeZ200 or by emailing hello@merchient.com. Either way, cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period: the brand keeps Vera until then, and no further payment is taken.

7. Risk review, not legal advice

Merchient provides risk review, not legal advice. The brand stays responsible for its ads, including what it runs and what it claims. Meta and the regulators make the final call.

A lawyer has not read these terms.

8. What Vera never does

  • Vera never promises approval. Merchient names the rule a line trips and does not promise how an ad will fare, or that an account is never restricted: Meta acts for reasons beyond claims.
  • Vera never acts in the brand’s accounts. She never posts, edits or pauses anything: she reads, and she drafts. The brand makes every change to its ads. If the brand has shared its ad account with Merchient, it can remove that access at any time in Meta Business Suite.
  • Vera does not read Amazon listings. Amazon is not covered.

9. Confidentiality

We keep the brand’s ad account data, reports, case files and emails confidential. We use them only to provide the service, share them only with the providers listed in our privacy policy that process data for us, and disclose them otherwise only when the law requires it. We do not name the brand as a customer, or quote it, except as the brand chose in the founding exchange (section 3) or with its permission.

10. Liability

To the extent the law allows, Merchient’s total liability for any claim relating to the service is limited to the fees the brand paid Merchient in the last three months before the claim.
