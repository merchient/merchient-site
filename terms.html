Terms of service

Last updated 13 October 2026

These terms are between Orbline LLC, trading as Merchient, 30 N Gould St, STE R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA (“Merchient”, “we”), and the brand that subscribes (“the brand”).

1. The service

Merchient is for US supplement brands running Meta ads. Vera is Merchient’s claims employee. She is an AI employee. A person checks every report before it reaches the brand.

The brand gets:

  1. a free first report before paying anything: every live ad, landing page and review checked; each risky claim quoted, with who would act on it (Meta, the FTC or the FDA) and a safe rewrite that keeps the hook;
  2. a brief from Vera every morning: new or changed risks, or a one-line all-clear;
  3. ad checks before launch: any new ad sent to Vera gets a verdict and a safe rewrite the same day;
  4. every week, what changed in Meta, FTC and FDA rules and what it means for the brand’s ads;
  5. bonus: a competitor claims watch, showing which claims rivals run and which ones disappeared;
  6. a person checks every report.

The free first report reads only the brand’s public ads and pages, so it needs no access. After the brand joins, it takes two minutes to share the ad account with Merchient, view-only. Nothing to log in to, no software. Merchient delivers the full first report within 48 hours of connecting, counted from the share.

2. Founding terms

Founding brands pay $1,500 a month; the standard price is $3,000. Locked for 12 months while the subscription runs. Month to month, cancel any time. 5 founding places, because a person reviews every report. In return for the founding price: an honest testimonial after 30 days, if the brand is happy.

3. Guarantees

30 days money back, no questions asked, once per brand.

Rejection guarantee: if Meta rejects one of the brand’s ads for a claim Vera reviewed (in a morning brief or a check before launch) and did not flag, that month’s fee is refunded or credited, the brand’s choice; it applies when the ad that ran is the text and media Vera reviewed and Meta’s notice names a claim-related policy; a claim Vera flagged, at any tier, that ran anyway is not covered.

To use either guarantee, email hello@merchient.com.

4. Billing

Billing is monthly, in advance, through Stripe.

5. Cancellation

The brand can cancel at any time by emailing hello@merchient.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month: the service continues until then, and no further payment is taken.

6. Not legal advice

Merchient provides risk review, not legal advice. The brand stays responsible for its ads, including what it runs and what it claims. There is no promise that an account is never restricted, because Meta acts for reasons beyond claims.

7. Vera never acts in the brand’s accounts

Vera never posts, edits or pauses anything in the brand’s accounts: she reads, and she drafts. The brand makes every change to its ads. The brand can remove Merchient’s access at any time in Meta Business Settings.

We use them only to provide the service, share them only with the providers listed in our privacy policy that process data for us, and disclose them otherwise only when the law requires it.
