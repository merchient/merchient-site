Terms for the 48-Hour Claims Audit and Vera (draft)

Draft, site 49, 4 November 2026, from the offer decided that day. Not linked, not in the sitemap, not in force; the terms in force are at terms.html. Bracketed text is open.

1. The 48-Hour Claims Audit

Merchient reads every live ad and page the brand runs and delivers, within 48 hours, a numbered case file: each Red or Yellow line, with the rule it trips in plain words and a safer line that keeps the sell; the category’s Winning Lines; and the brand’s Claim Gap. Read by Vera, checked by a person. [48 hours from payment, or from the order email? Open.]

2. The audit’s price

One price per brand, by live ads on the day of the order: $450 up to 25; $750 for 26 to 100; $1,200 for more than 100. Paid at order.

3. The audit’s refund

If the audit finds nothing Red or Yellow, the price is refunded in full, and the brand keeps the Winning Lines.

4. The credit

The audit’s price counts in full toward the first month of Vera when the brand starts Vera within 14 days of the audit. [14 days from delivery or from payment? Open.]

5. Vera, your claims employee

Every new ad and page pre-cleared before it runs; same-day rescues and appeals; the weekly watch; Winning Lines and the Claim Gap every month; a launch-day pre-clear for every new product; a 20-minute call with the founder every month. Every brand starts with the audit.

6. The founding ten

$1,500 a month for the first ten brands, kept for twelve months while the brand stays. In exchange, the brand gives a case file (named or anonymous, its choice) and one sentence Merchient may quote; a quoted sentence carries the founding price next to it. Founding closes at ten brands or on 30 November 2026, whichever comes first.

7. After the ten

$2,500 a month.

8. Guarantees

Founding brands: cancel within 30 days of the first payment and that payment is refunded in full; once per brand.

Every Vera brand, the rescue guarantee: a pulled ad or a restricted account on a claims reason gets its rewrite and appeal within one US business day, or that month is refunded. [How the business day is counted, and what shows “a claims reason”: open.]

9. Billing and cancelling

Vera is billed monthly, in advance, through Stripe, and renews each month until the brand cancels. Cancel any time, in the billing portal or by emailing hello@merchient.com. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid month: the brand keeps Vera until then, and no further payment is taken. [A founding brand that leaves and returns: founding or standard price? Open.]

10. Not covered

Amazon listings and accounts. Legal advice: this is risk review, not legal advice. No promise that Meta runs an ad or restores an account; Meta decides, for reasons beyond claims.

11. Agencies

The same two, per client brand. The first three client brands at an agency are at the founding price. No percentage, no white label.
